# Product brief

## Problem
Daughter (2nd grade, RSM) struggles with addition and subtraction while the class has moved on to multiplication. Worksheets feel like a chore.

## Users
One child player (7-8), one parent checking progress.

## Scope (v1)
- 4 worlds, 21 levels, from number bonds to 10 up to x2/x5/x10.
- Visual models RSM uses: ten-frames, number bonds, number lines, base-ten blocks, equal groups, arrays.
- Hints with a picture on the first mistake, worked answer on the second, and the fact comes back later.
- Rewards: stars, coins, streaks, sticker book, boss battles, confetti, sounds, read-aloud.
- "Power-up practice" built from the facts she missed.
- Grown-ups corner (behind a math question): accuracy by skill, most-missed facts, unlock/reset.
- Launch from a desktop icon, works offline.

## Out of scope
Accounts, cloud sync, timed drills, ads.

## Success looks like
She asks to play; addition/subtraction within 20 above 90% first-try accuracy in the Grown-ups corner.

## Next
