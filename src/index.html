🔒 Privacy

Math Quest has no accounts and no ads. Your child's name, progress and play statistics are stored only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

If a grown-up turns on the optional device sync, progress is also kept in a private cloud save that only someone with your sync code can open.

To erase everything: Grown-ups corner → Reset all progress, or clear this site's data in your browser.
